# Specific creature facts — 13 October 2026

Update, 1 November 2026: the revised Day Octopus artwork now represents *Octopus cyanea*. Its row below supersedes the earlier greater blue-ringed octopus identification and measurements. The card retains the ID `octopus`.

This follow-up replaces the broad-group “Varies” policy in the 5 October review. The user chose representative species matching the artwork and asked to retain weight, using supported estimates where available.

Updated 47 master card designs (37 creature profiles, including all 15 school stages), four affected holo printings, and the two existing affected export frames. Family remains the leftmost fact. Card titles, IDs, game rules, and illustrations are preserved. The gallery supplies the scientific name, representative species, and a short measurement note.

The gallery uses 47 additional editable preview frames on the existing Gallery — reviewed previews page. Each preserves the previously published card design as a background and supplies native text for the reviewed facts and description. The saved backgrounds were verified against every pre-update gallery image hash. This avoids overlapping layers and missing backgrounds in full-card exports from the current file. The full master cards remain editable and contain the same corrected facts. Temporary export pages were removed after the final previews were saved.

Measurements are specific reference values, not a uniform series of maximum records. A tilde (~) marks approximations, rounded sample means, or model estimates. Length labels identify the measured structure. School measurements describe one individual. Where maximum lengths and mean weights come from different samples, the gallery says so explicitly. Model-based fish weights are estimates, not newly measured records.

Representative identities were checked against WoRMS; the downloaded records are in artifacts/creature-facts-specific/representative-taxonomy.json. Pacific sardine uses the accepted family Alosidae.

## Remaining evidence gaps

Seven weights remain Unknown: Giant Phantom Jelly, Gulper Eel, Nudibranch (Chromodoris magnifica), Deep Sea Jelly (Atolla wyvillei), Dumbo Octopus, Blue Sea Dragon (Glaucus atlanticus), and Barrel Eye Fish (Macropinna microstoma). The review did not locate defensible whole-body weights for these entries. This is a limitation of the reviewed evidence; it does not imply that all seven animals are extraordinarily elusive. In particular, the magnificent chromodoris is not being described as elusive. Unrelated species weights, specimen shipping weights, and unsourced online guesses were not substituted.
