# Creature fact review — 5 October 2026

The [13 October specificity review](creature-fact-specificity-review.md) supersedes the broad-group and school measurement policy below, using representative species and supported numeric estimates.

Reviewed the five creature pages in the SeaRealm Figma file (136 card designs, including school stages), plus the legacy Southern Flounder gallery preview. Coral, habitat, and support cards are separate categories and were not part of this review.

The first natural-history field is now the taxonomic family. Broad cards spanning several families use **Family varies**, rather than an invented species identification. Taxonomy follows the current World Register of Marine Species; notable updates include Galeocerdonidae for tiger sharks, Epinephelidae for groupers, Inachoididae for arrow crabs, and Labridae/Scarinae for parrotfish.

Sizes are rounded reference values, not a uniform series of maximum records. Width, shell, bell, spine, arm, and tentacle labels distinguish measurements that were previously all labeled length. **Unknown** replaces weights that could not be reliably established in this review. **Varies** is used for broad groups and changing schools. The numerical sizes and masses formerly assigned to whole schools were not treated as measured facts.

Descriptions were checked against the species sources linked below, with taxonomy responses and source snapshots retained in artifacts/creature-facts. The main corrections include the copied 65-foot Blue Sea Dragon and Market Squid sizes; Arctic versus Southern Ocean for Colossal Squid; Indo-Pacific cleaner wrasses and cleaner shrimp; Spanish Hogfish size; megamouth light reflection rather than bioluminescence; and unsupported top-speed claims.

The six older layouts received a fourth fact column. Southern Flounder had no remaining editable source, so its existing preview was preserved in a new Figma frame with an editable family label. Game rules, costs, dice, and collector information were preserved. The website text uses src/data/creature-facts.json so it stays consistent with the card descriptions.

All 29 holo creature printings were checked separately and updated to match the reviewed master facts. Final readback checks passed for all 136 master designs and all 29 holo printings.

The website received 124 refreshed card previews. Four artwork-heavy cards (Lionfish, Blue Tang, Frogfish, and Sea Urchin) use their existing artwork with the corrected, editable Figma template text in lightweight export frames. Their original master designs also contain the corrections. Those frames and the restored Southern Flounder preview are retained on the **Gallery — reviewed previews** page; temporary export pages were removed.

Validation included visual inspection of the refreshed layouts, the gallery manifest check, 20 targeted content tests, and a successful production build. Published to [the live gallery](https://searealm.com/gallery) on 5 October 2026 (America/New_York), Cloudflare version `7e9ad103-19f0-4ce0-8cdd-ffcaceffbe05`. Post-deployment checks confirmed five public pages, all 11 gallery JavaScript assets, and all 124 refreshed images matched the release. Detailed verification records are retained in artifacts/creature-facts.
