# SeaPals Tutorial Video Director Script

Target length: 4-6 minutes

Audience: New players learning their first game

Tone: Clear, friendly, lightly energetic, focused on showing the table state

## Production Notes

- Keep the cards large enough to read whenever a rule is introduced.
- Use quick labels for important terms: VP, RP, Foundation Deck, Pals Deck, Conditions Deck, Slots, Attack.
- Show one running example board throughout the video instead of resetting the table for every rule.
- When explaining attacks, cut to dice and card icons rather than staying on the full table.
- Avoid explaining every card type in detail. Teach the rhythm, then point viewers to the full rules for edge cases.

## Props And Table Setup

- Two player areas, each with a Foundation Deck, Pals Deck, discard area, RP bank, and visible ecosystem.
- One shared Conditions Deck in the center.
- Example coral foundation cards, including Base, Stage 1, and Stage 2.
- Example Pals: a fish, predator, apex, invertebrate, and one bait ball or school card.
- Dice reference or real dice: D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, D20.
- Tokens or counters for RP and bait ball HP.

## Script

### 0:00-0:15 - Cold Open

Shot: Fast tabletop montage. Cards slide into place, RP tokens enter the bank, a condition flips, dice roll, then a completed ecosystem fills the frame.

Narration:
"Welcome to SeaPals, a trading card game where you build a living ocean ecosystem, grow your foundation, play creatures into the right habitats, and race to the Victory Point goal."

On-screen text:
"Build your ecosystem. Protect your VP. Reach the goal first."

### 0:15-0:40 - How You Win

Shot: Close-up of a Pal card with VP visible. Then pull back to show several VP cards in play.

Narration:
"The goal is simple: be the first player to reach the agreed Victory Point target with cards currently in play. A full game is usually played to 30 VP. For a quick game, try 10 VP."

Shot: Remove one VP card from the board and lower the visible VP count.

Narration:
"But VP only counts while the card stays in your ecosystem. If a VP card leaves play, those points leave with it."

On-screen text:
"Full game: 30 VP"
"Quick game: 10 VP"
"VP must be in play"

### 0:40-1:20 - Setup

Shot: Overhead view of the three deck areas.

Narration:
"Each player starts with two personal decks. Your Foundation Deck is your habitat engine. It includes cards like coral, coral upgrades, and school foundations. Your Pals Deck contains creatures, support cards, and playable habitats."

Shot: Place the shared Conditions Deck between players.

Narration:
"There is also one shared Conditions Deck. Conditions are revealed round by round and affect everyone."

Shot: Deal four Foundation cards, four Pals cards, and place three RP tokens in a bank.

Narration:
"To start, draw four cards from your Foundation Deck and four from your Pals Deck. Begin with 3 RP in your RP bank, then spend setup RP to play a valid starting foundation. If you cannot play one, redraw your Foundation hand."

On-screen text:
"Start: 4 Foundation, 4 Pals, 3 RP"

### 1:20-1:55 - Reading Cards

Shot: Close-up of a Pal card. Highlight type icon, cost, VP, abilities, and defense.

Narration:
"Most cards use the same basic language. Look for the type icon, the card name, RP cost, Victory Points, abilities, and defense."

Shot: Close-up of a coral card. Highlight RP production, HP or foundation details, weaknesses, and slots.

Narration:
"Foundation cards, like coral, show what they produce and what they support. Coral is especially important because it produces RP and creates slots where your reef or deep Pals can live."

On-screen text:
"RP = resource points"
"VP = victory points"

### 1:55-2:35 - Turn Flow

Shot: Four clean title cards or tabletop cards labeled Choose, Collect, Build, Attack.

Narration:
"Each turn follows four steps: Choose, Collect, Build, and Attack."

Shot: Player chooses between the two personal decks.

Narration:
"First, Choose: draw from either your Foundation Deck or your Pals Deck."

Shot: Add one RP, then add RP from active foundations.

Narration:
"Next, Collect: gain 1 RP, then collect any RP your active foundations produce."

Shot: Player pays RP and plays a card into the ecosystem.

Narration:
"Then Build: play cards, upgrade foundations, and use paid actions."

Shot: Attacking card turns toward a target.

Narration:
"Finally, Attack: use available attacks one at a time against legal targets."

On-screen text:
"1. Choose"
"2. Collect"
"3. Build"
"4. Attack"

### 2:35-3:25 - Slots And Habitats

Shot: Show a coral card with slots, then place a matching Pal onto or near the correct slot.

Narration:
"Slots are habitat-specific. A reef slot holds reef Pals. A deep slot holds deep Pals. Matching the habitat matters, even if two cards are the same broad type."

Shot: Place a Deep Fish into a Deep Fish slot. Then show a Reef Fish hovering near the same slot with an X overlay.

Narration:
"For example, a Deep Fish can enter a Deep Fish slot. A Reef Fish cannot enter that slot, because it belongs to a different habitat."

Shot: Show fish, predator, and apex slot progression.

Narration:
"Some larger slots can also hold smaller eligible classes in the same habitat. A predator slot can hold fish or predators. An apex slot can hold fish, predators, or apex creatures."

On-screen text:
"Habitat must match"
"Predator slot: Fish or Predator"
"Apex slot: Fish, Predator, or Apex"

### 3:25-4:00 - Coral, Upgrades, And Conditions

Shot: Base coral upgrades to Stage 1, then Stage 2.

Narration:
"Coral can grow by stages. To upgrade, you need the next stage in hand and enough RP to pay the upgrade cost. Each coral can upgrade only once per turn."

Shot: Flip a Conditions card such as Hurricane, Red Tide, or another visible condition.

Narration:
"At the start of each round, reveal a Conditions card. Conditions can change the table for everyone."

Shot: Show coral weakness icon matching a condition icon. Remove or cover RP production for that round.

Narration:
"If a condition matches a coral weakness, that coral does not produce RP this round. The coral stays in play, but its production is shut off temporarily."

On-screen text:
"Upgrade: next stage + RP"
"One upgrade per coral each turn"
"Weaknesses can stop RP production"

### 4:00-5:00 - Combat Example

Shot: Close-up of an attack indicator. Highlight die, legal target icons, and attack count.

Narration:
"To attack, read the attack indicator. It tells you which die to roll, what types of cards can be targeted, and how many attacks are made."

Shot: Spinner Dolphins, or another predator, targets a fish. Show legal target icons.

Narration:
"Choose a legal target that matches the attack icons. Then the attacker rolls its attack die, and the defender rolls its defense die."

Shot: Roll attacker D8 and defender D6. Show attacker winning.

Narration:
"The attacker must roll higher. Ties go to the defender. If the attacker succeeds, the defending creature is discarded."

Shot: Show a bait ball or school card with HP tokens.

Narration:
"Creature Schools and bait balls work differently. They do not roll defense. Instead, damage equals the attack roll times 10. When their HP reaches zero, discard them."

Shot: Show repeated attack count, then choose different targets.

Narration:
"If an attack repeats, resolve each attack separately. The same target cannot be chosen twice in that sequence."

On-screen text:
"Attacker must roll higher"
"Tie = defender wins"
"Bait ball damage = roll x 10"

### 5:00-5:30 - Endgame And Quick Recap

Shot: Player counts VP in play and reaches the target.

Narration:
"Keep building, protecting, and attacking until one player reaches the Victory Point target with cards in play. If you ever must draw and both your Foundation Deck and Pals Deck are empty, you lose."

Shot: Return to the four-step turn flow.

Narration:
"That is the core loop: choose a deck, collect RP, build your ecosystem, and attack. Once you understand slots, RP, VP, and legal targets, you are ready to play SeaPals."

On-screen text:
"Choose. Collect. Build. Attack."
"First to the VP goal wins."

### 5:30-5:45 - Closing

Shot: Hero spread of colorful SeaPals cards, dice, and a finished board.

Narration:
"Grab your decks, reveal the first condition, and start building your ocean. Welcome to SeaPals."

On-screen text:
"Play SeaPals"

## Optional Short Version

For a 60-90 second social cut:

1. Open with the ecosystem montage.
2. Explain the win condition: reach 10 or 30 VP with cards in play.
3. Show setup: Foundation Deck, Pals Deck, Conditions Deck, 4 cards each, 3 RP.
4. Teach the turn loop: Choose, Collect, Build, Attack.
5. Show one slot example and one attack example.
6. End with "First to the VP goal wins."

## Pickup Shot Checklist

- Close-up of VP on several cards.
- RP tokens entering and leaving the bank.
- Foundation Deck and Pals Deck draw choices.
- Shared Conditions Deck reveal.
- Coral base-to-stage upgrade sequence.
- Matching and non-matching slot examples.
- Attack indicator close-up.
- Dice roll comparison with attacker higher.
- Dice roll comparison with defender winning a tie.
- Bait ball HP damage example.
- Final VP count reaching the target.
