SIMPLE LETTER STAPLING TRAY WITH GLUE ALIGNMENT TABS

FILES / QUANTITY
paper-stapling-rig.blend: assembled tray and two separate tab objects.
paper-stapling-rig.stl: print one tray, flat underside down.
glue-alignment-tab.stl: print two identical tabs, flat face down.
STL coordinates are millimeters; import at 100% scale.

DIMENSIONS
Letter paper: 215.9 x 279.4 mm (8.5 x 11 inches).
Glue sheet: 209.55 x 273.05 mm (8.25 x 10.75 inches).
Tab inward edges: nominally 273.05 mm (10.75 inches) apart.
Glue end inset: 3.175 mm (1/8 inch) from each paper end.
Gap under each tab: 1.0 mm above the tray surface.
Each tab: 44 x 15.375 x 3 mm.
Tray outer footprint including mounts: 222.8 x 306.3 mm
  (approximately 8.77 x 12.06 inches).
Paper surface remains 12.7 mm (1/2 inch) above the tabletop.
Tray rim height: 15.7 mm. Assembled tab height: 16.7 mm, excluding hardware.
Stapler slots remain 38.7 mm wide.
Check the actual printer's usable build area before printing the one-piece tray.
This footprint is larger than a 256 x 256 mm build plate.

HARDWARE / ASSEMBLY
Four M3 x 20 socket-head bolts and four M3 nuts.
Insert bolts upward from the underside of the tray; put nuts above the tabs.
The tray has 3.4 mm through-holes and 6.4 mm diameter x 3.4 mm deep
underside head recesses. Check actual bolt heads fit fully above the tabletop
plane so the tray retains the correct stapler height. Deburr holes as needed.
The two tabs are identical; rotate the second 180 degrees in the tray plane.
Their inward edges face the center of the tray.

FIRST-TIME SETUP
1. Leave the nuts just snug enough to allow small tab adjustments.
2. Seat a Letter sheet against the TOP and RIGHT stops. In Blender, top is +Y
   and right is +X; keep this registration consistent between stacks.
3. Place the glue sheet centered on that sheet, with 3.175 mm at both ends.
4. Look straight down at each end and align the inward tab edge to the glue
   sheet edge. Hold the tab square while tightening both nuts; recheck.
   Screw holes have clearance, so calibrate before tightening rather than
   assuming the bolts alone position the tabs precisely.
5. Verify the complete three-sheet stack slides freely under both tabs.
   If needed, place equal washer shims between the tabs and mounting ears.
   Do not add washers below the recessed bolt heads.

USE
Seat the bottom Letter sheet against TOP and RIGHT. Visually align the glue
sheet's top and bottom edges with the inward tab edges. Then place and seat
the top Letter sheet without shifting the glue sheet, and staple.
These are visual references above the sheets, not physical glue-sheet stops.
They establish top/bottom alignment only; check left/right centering separately.

VERIFICATION
All three modeled parts are closed solids with one connected component each.
Mesh measurements checked reference spacing, 1 mm paper clearance, bore
alignment, original paper pocket, bed height, and staple-slot widths.
Fit has not yet been checked with a physical print or the actual hardware.
