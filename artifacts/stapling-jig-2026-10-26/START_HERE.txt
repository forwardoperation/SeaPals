measured replica. Paper, glue, screws and stapler are reference objects only;
they are not included in the STL exports.

HOW IT WORKS
Outer sheets register against two right-edge pads and one top-right pad.
The teal U guide opens to the right and withdraws LEFT. Its thick frame rides
in recessed tracks outside the paper; thin fingers locate the glue inside it.
Seat the guide to the RIGHT ends of its tracks and bias it toward the TOP track
wall. This repeatable seated position establishes the smaller sheet's offset.
Press the glue gently against the TOP and LEFT fingers. The bottom fingers
have 0.2 mm extra clearance, so they are guards rather than the primary datum.
Two right-side screw clamps grip all three sheets before the guide is withdrawn.
Teal loading bridges support paper over the stapler openings during assembly.
After clamping, remove the guide first, then pull both loading bridges left.
The lower stapler base slides between adjustable orange rails to an orange toe
stop. The metal anvil, rather than printed plastic, supports the staple itself.

DAILY SEQUENCE
1. Fit both loading bridges. Leave the stapler out. Back off the hold-down screws.
2. Place the 8.5 x 11 inch bottom sheet against the TOP and RIGHT paper datums.
3. Seat the U guide fully right and toward its top track wall.
4. Lay the 8.25 x 10.75 inch dry glue sheet inside; gently bias it TOP and LEFT.
5. Place the top 8.5 x 11 inch sheet against the TOP and RIGHT paper datums.
6. Position the two loose pressure pads and gently tighten both hold-down screws.
7. Pull the U guide straight LEFT about 200 mm, keeping it level.
8. Pull both loading bridges LEFT. Keep the stack clamped.
9. Slide the stapler into the first dock until it reaches its calibrated stop;
   press the stapler, withdraw it, then repeat at the other station.
10. Release the clamps and remove the stack. Heat activation happens separately;
    this is a room-temperature alignment fixture, not part of the heating press.

CONFIRMED / DERIVED DIMENSIONS
Outer paper: 215.9 x 279.4 mm (8.5 x 11 inches).
Glue sheet: 209.55 x 273.05 mm (8.25 x 10.75 inches).
Nominal centered border: 3.175 mm (1/8 inch) on all four sides.
Stapler base width: approximately 38.1 mm (1.5 inches), supplied by user.
Staple targets: 5.4187 mm from LEFT; 49.403 and 233.807 mm from TOP.
Targets derive from Figma dashed-box centers, assuming actual-size Letter print:
2550 x 3300 source pixels at 300 pixels/inch; boxes x25, y468/2646, w78, h231.
The glue offset is nominal: actual trim variation and printer scaling must be
checked on physical sheets. Mechanical registration cannot correct artwork
printed at a different scale or position on the page.

MEASURE BEFORE FULL PRINT
A. Base width at TWO separated positions along the rigid lower base.
B. Toe-to-staple distance: from the very front tip of the lower base BACK to
   the center line of the metal anvil/staple strike. Default 18 mm is unmeasured.
C. Anvil height: tabletop to the top surface of the metal anvil, with the stapler
   sitting normally on its rubber feet. Default 12 mm is unmeasured.
D. Actual bottom paper and glue thickness. Defaults 0.20 and 0.15 mm are guesses.

The paper bed is 4 mm dock floor + anvil height = 16 mm in this prototype.
Toe stop X = 5.4187 mm staple inset + measured toe-to-staple distance.
The current low rails and loading bridges require anvil height above 10.2 mm.
For a lower stapler, revise those parts rather than just changing the bed height.
The current stop slot gives roughly -2.6 to +7.8 mm adjustment around the modeled
position with an M3 shank; exact usable travel depends on washer and clearance.
If the actual toe distance falls outside this range, regenerate the stop/dock.

HARDWARE / BACKING
- One flat, rigid backing board, approximately 425 x 320 x 9 mm.
  Model board bounds: X -180..245; Y -20..300. Board is not an STL part.
- 12 M3 bolts for rails/toe stops (M3x8 starting size), nuts and washers.
- Four M3x25 bolts, nuts and washers for the two hold-down feet.
- Two M4 hex nuts (standard 7 mm across flats) and two M4x30 thumbscrews.
- Adhesive suited to your printed material for seam keys and guide splice plates.
- Smooth protective tape for the pressure-pad undersides if needed.
- Suitable short board screws for the eight 3.6 mm dock mounting bores.

Board hardware relief is essential: the M3 nuts and bolt tips extend beneath
the printed floors. The CSV gives 10 mm THROUGH relief holes measured from the
board's left/bottom edges. These permit nut/bolt access; THEY ARE NOT LOCATORS.
Fit underside nuts before fixing the pieces down. Verify actual bolt lengths
and washer thickness; no bolt or nut may lift the printed assembly off the board.
The M4 nuts fit into pockets on the UNDERSIDE of the clamp arms, with solid
plastic above them taking the clamping reaction. Insert before mounting the arms.

ASSEMBLY
1. Print one width coupon first. The three channels are 38.4, 38.7 and 39.1 mm.
   Use it to test the real rigid base, not the moving upper cover. Trim/measure
   actual sliding clearance before committing to large parts.
2. Join the four tray tiles on a flat surface using four underside keys. Seams
   are butt joints; key clearance permits assembly. Align TOP/RIGHT paper datum
   faces and verify the full paper footprint before fixing the joints.
3. Join each pair of dock floors with two underside keys (four keys total).
4. Assemble the four U-guide sections with three keyed splice plates. Glue both
   the butt joints and plate interfaces on a flat surface. Pegs only align the
   plates; no guide screws/nuts are needed. Keep every paper-contact edge smooth.
5. Dry-fit the guide in its tray tracks. Seat it RIGHT and TOP. Measure its
   glue-contact positions before fixing anything permanently. It must slide
   out freely without rocking, lifting paper or allowing the film underneath.
6. Secure the tray firmly to the backing board with suitable adhesive or thin
   uniform adhesive tape. Align and secure both docks to the SAME board: each
   front edge X=42 meets the back of its tray opening, and each 88 mm dock is
   centered in the corresponding opening. Rails must be perpendicular to the
   paper's left edge. The tray itself MUST be fixed; arm bolts do not do this.
7. Install adjustable rails/stops using the board relief holes for nut access.
   Fit loading bridges and verify their surfaces are flush with the paper bed.
8. Install clamps with the M4 nuts underneath the arms. Use broad loose pads
   and only enough pressure to prevent sheet slip.
9. Calibrate both docks independently with scrap paper. Set smooth rail travel,
   consistent side bias, correct anvil height, square entry and actual staple
   centers. Tighten adjustment bolts, then repeat the check several times.

PRINTING
STL coordinates are millimeters. Import at 100% scale; check size against manifest.
Every part's exported footprint is at most 174 mm, leaving only 6 mm total on a
180 mm plate. Check your actual Bambu model, slicer margins and brim requirements.
Exported guide sections are inverted so thin finger surfaces lie on the bed.
Splice plates export flat face down with pegs upward. Clamp arms export on side.
Loading bridges export upright: they need supports under the loading surfaces,
or a deliberate alternative orientation. Other parts should be checked in slicer.
0.2 mm layers produce three layers in the 0.6 mm fingers. As starting settings,
use four walls and moderate infill; stiff, flat parts and smooth tracks matter
more than a particular infill number. Inspect thin-finger toolpaths before printing.

CALIBRATION / ACCEPTANCE
- Confirm a 100 mm model distance prints as 100 mm. Do not scale to fit the plate.
- Verify BOTH printed outer sheets touch their datums without curling or buckling.
- Verify actual dry glue film catches the TOP and LEFT fingers. The modeled
  finger-bottom gap above bottom paper is only 0.05 mm; printer Z rounding can
  consume that margin. Tune track depth/guide seating or deburr/shim as needed.
- Check the glue cannot ride on the fingers, slip underneath, or move when the
  guide is pulled. Use light clamp pressure and remove burrs before testing.
- Check bridge withdrawal and stapler insertion do not lift or drag the stack.
- Staple sacrificial stacks at both stations. Compare actual centers with the
  printed boxes; adjust the stops using the actual result, not the online listing.
- Repeat on at least ten stacks and measure all four glue borders. Record the
  spread and decide whether it meets your manufacturing tolerance.

DIGITAL VALIDATION
All 38 exported part meshes are closed, positive-volume, single connected solids.
Nominal assembled printed solids have no detected positive-volume overlaps.
Guide withdrawal was checked at -1,-5,-15,-30,-60,-100,-150,-200 mm.
Bridge withdrawal was checked at -1,-10,-30,-60,-100,-200 mm with guide removed.
These checks exclude physical tolerance, exact hardware/stapler shape, flex and
friction. They are not evidence of achieved manufacturing repeatability.
